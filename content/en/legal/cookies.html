--- title: "Cookie policy" slug: "cookies" description: "anisakis.com does not use cookies." ---

This website does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies, whether first-party or third-party. Nor does it include analytics, advertising or social media tools, embedded videos or external fonts.

That is why no cookie notice is displayed: there is nothing to accept or configure.

The only exception is the news editing area (/admin/), used only by the people who write the news: it stores the session in their browser’s local storage and connects to GitHub and to code delivery services in order to work. It does not affect visitors to the website.

Should any cookie that is not strictly necessary be introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before it is used.
