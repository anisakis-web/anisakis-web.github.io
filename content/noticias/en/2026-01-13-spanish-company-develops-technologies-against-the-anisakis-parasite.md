A Galician company based in Vigo, [**MAREXI**](https://marexi.com/), has placed itself at the global forefront of the fight against anisakis thanks to the development of unique technologies aimed at **improving food safety**.

Marexi has designed the **only equipment in the world validated by the CSIC** that can be used directly on board fishing vessels to **neutralise the parasites** present in fish viscera during gutting and cleaning on board. This advance marks a milestone, as it **acts at the source of the problem**, before anisakis can re-enter the food chain. The spread of the parasite in the marine environment is effectively reduced.

In addition to this product, Marexi has developed the **only industrial scanner capable of detecting anisakis in real time**, automatically **analysing** the fish and **separating** contaminated product from parasite-free product, significantly improving quality and safety controls in processing plants.

With this set of developments, Marexi not only strengthens consumer safety but also provides the fishing and food sectors with effective tools to combat one of the main health problems associated with eating fish.

Innovation born in Galicia is thus consolidating its position as an international benchmark in anisakis prevention.
