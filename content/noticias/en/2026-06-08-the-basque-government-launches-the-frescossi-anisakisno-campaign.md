The Basque Government's Departamento de Alimentación, Desarrollo Rural, Agricultura y Pesca has launched a new institutional initiative under the slogan **«#FrescosSíAnisakisNo»**. The main purpose of this informative and practical campaign is to boost the consumption of fresh fish while guaranteeing the highest level of food safety, as well as to strengthen public confidence in a product that is a cornerstone both of a healthy diet and of the Basque Country's gastronomic identity.

Regional minister Amaia Barredo stressed that fish is a safe and essential food, explaining that the campaign aims to give the public simple tools so they can continue to enjoy it with complete peace of mind. The campaign points out that avoiding the risks associated with the parasite does not require giving up fresh fish, but rather applying a series of basic habits when handling and preparing it.

## Key prevention guidelines promoted by the campaign

To keep the parasite under control in domestic and commercial settings, the initiative's recommendations are organised around the following informative themes:

- **Proper, prompt gutting:** Removing the viscera from the fish as soon as possible after it is caught or bought drastically reduces the risk of anisakis larvae migrating into the fish's muscle.
- **Correct cooking:** Cooking the pieces at the right temperature fully guarantees the elimination of the parasite and the safety of the dish.
- **Freezing for raw preparations:** When fish is to be eaten without prior heating (as in sushi, ceviche or marinades), the product must first be frozen for the time and at the temperature required to neutralise any larvae.

## Roll-out across the sector and technical support

The campaign has a direct dissemination strategy that will be carried out in fishmongers throughout the Basque Autonomous Community. Educational material aimed at both professionals in the sector and end consumers will be distributed in these establishments. The roll-out will be complemented by inserts and messages in the print press, on radio and on digital platforms.

This action has been directly promoted by the Basque Government's Dirección de Pesca y Acuicultura, working in close collaboration with the regional Departamento de Salud. The initiative also has specialist technical advice from **ELIKA** (the Fundación Vasca para la Seguridad Agroalimentaria), ensuring that all the guidelines communicated meet the highest scientific standards.
