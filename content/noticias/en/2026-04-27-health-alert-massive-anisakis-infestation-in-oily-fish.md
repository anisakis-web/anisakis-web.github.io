The Rapid Alert System for Food and Feed (RASFF) has issued a **serious risk** notification after detecting a massive presence of Anisakis larvae in a batch of fish sold in Spain. The authorities have already begun withdrawing the product, but consumers are asked to take the utmost care.

### Key points of the alert

- **Situation:** An unusually high parasite load was detected during official controls.
- **Risk:** Ingesting live larvae can cause **anisakiasis** (gastric lesions) or severe allergic reactions (even if the parasite is dead).
- **Product:** Mainly domestically sourced oily fish distributed through various chains.

### Prevention measures

To ensure complete safety, strictly apply these three rules:

- **Prompt gutting:** Buy the fish cleaned or remove the viscera as soon as possible to prevent the parasite from passing into the muscle.
- **Safety freezing:** If you are going to eat the fish raw, semi-cooked or pickled in vinegar, you must freeze it at **-20 °C** for a minimum of **5 days**.
- **High heat:** For everyday consumption, make sure you cook the fish at over **60 °C** (for at least 1 minute at the centre of the piece). If the flesh is not opaque and firm, it is not ready.

### What to avoid?

- Do not rely on methods such as the microwave or quick griddling if the piece is thick; the heat must be even.
- Avoid home-made preparations of anchovies in vinegar or sushi without first freezing the fish for 5 days.
